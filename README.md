# 🏥 Hospital Operations & Capacity Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
An end-to-end interactive Power BI solution designed to help healthcare administrators monitor patient throughput, evaluate hospital bed capacity bottlenecks, and analyze workforce attendance and morale metrics.

---

## 📊 Key Pages & Features

### 1. Patient Demographics & Trends
* **Patient Tracking:** Monitored total vs. unique patient volume and latest arrival/departure dates.
* **Monthly Patient Trends:** Visualized month-over-month patient arrival volumes.
* **Age Binned Analysis:** Categorized patients into structured age brackets (`0–19`, `20–39`, `40–59`, `60–79`, `80+`) for targeted demographic insights.
* **Department Satisfaction:** Analyzed average satisfaction scores across major departments (Surgery, ICU, Emergency, General Medicine).

### 2. Capacity & Refusal Analysis
* **Bed Availability:** Tracked weekly available beds trend across different hospital events (flu season, strikes, donations).
* **Refusal Metrics:** Monitored total and peak patient refusal counts alongside average patient satisfaction to identify operational bottlenecks.

### 3. Staffing & Workforce Management
* **Department & Role Breakdown:** Tracked staff counts across departments and job roles (Doctors, Nurses, Nursing Assistants).
* **Attendance Tracking:** Analyzed weekly attendance rates and overall morale scores to support workforce planning.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Skills
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL, conditional columns, data type cleaning)
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema relationship modeling, custom sort columns
* **DAX:** Measures for aggregations, conditional filtering, and custom binned logic
